Wire Loop – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Wire Loop: Don't Touch! is a mobile game made by Eren Turkut ("I", "me"). This policy explains what data the game uses and why.

1. Data stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, gold, cosmetics and settings) is saved only on your device. I do not collect it, and the game does not need an account.

2. Advertising

The game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay (Unity Ads and ironSource Ads) and Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, these companies may collect and use data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type and general location, and information about how you interact with ads. Depending on your choices, ads may be personalised or non-personalised.

Unity: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

Google: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

3. Google Play Games (optional)

If you use Google Play Games, the game shows your Google Play Games profile name and avatar, saves your Endless distance to the leaderboard and unlocks achievements. This data is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Notifications and ratings

The game can show reminder notifications (for example, when your daily reward is ready). They are created on your phone; no data is sent for them. You can turn them off in your phone's settings. The "rate this game" window is shown by Google Play.

5. Your choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > PRIVACY. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

8. Contact
